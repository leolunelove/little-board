# Using Little Board

[Open Little Board](https://littleboard.cc/) · [Back to the README](../README.md)

## Start a board

Choose **Create board**, give it a name, and add your first item. A guest board saves on this device without asking for an email.

Use **Pending** for work still to do, **Waiting** for something blocked or awaiting a response, and **Done** for finished work. Done items leave the active list after 24 hours and remain available in **Archived**.

## Work with items

Click an item to edit it inline. Add a short note, an assignment, or a checklist when useful. Use the circle to complete an item and the item menu to change its status or remove it. Undo appears briefly after completion, movement, or removal.

On an individual board, drag to reorder items or use the item menu’s movement controls. Quick add stays near the bottom of the screen, so you do not need to scroll to the end of a long board. Press Enter to add another item.

**Paste request** helps turn a pasted message into proposed tasks. Review the wording and grouping, remove anything unnecessary, then add the items. Board and List views offer two ways to read the same saved work.

## Work across boards

Open **All items** from Your boards, or bookmark [littleboard.cc/all/](https://littleboard.cc/all/).

- See active tasks from boards available to you, grouped by status.
- Search titles, notes, and checklist steps, or filter to one board.
- Edit tasks you own, complete them, and use Undo without opening each board.
- Choose the destination board in **Add to** when adding an item.
- Click the board name below a task to open that board. **All items** takes you back to your previous filter, search, and position.

Items remain on their original boards. Shared boards stay read-only, and reordering happens inside the individual board. The page refreshes periodically and when you return to the tab; refresh pauses while you are editing. Recent Done items are collapsed by default. Older completed work is available through each board’s Archived view.

## Save to your email

Choose **Save with email** on a guest board. Open the magic link in the same browser that requested it to complete sign-in. Once the board is saved to your email, sign in with that email on another device to reopen it.

Until a board is claimed, its only copy is in that browser’s storage. Clearing site data or changing browsers does not carry it over.

## Share with one person

Save the board to your email first. Use **Share board** to give one email address read access, then copy and send the board link yourself. The other person signs in with the invited email.

They can read the current state without seeing editing controls. The owner remains the only editor. Knowing the numeric board address does not grant access to a cloud board.

## A few quiet details

Light and night mode are available in the appearance control. Notes and checklists expand when you need the detail. Redaction is an optional visual effect for selected text; it does not secure or remove that text.
